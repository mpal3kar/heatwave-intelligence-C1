System Overview & Block Sections

1. System Objective

The main objective of this system is to continuously monitor real-time temperature data, calculate deviation trends, and issue early warnings before an extreme heatwave becomes dangerous to human health and urban stability.

2. Data Sources

Sensor telemetry is gathered around the clock from automatic weather stations (AWS), the centralized IMD national database, and regional thermal infrared satellite imagery feeds.

3. Target Stakeholders

The system is actively used by meteorologists for modeling, disaster management officers for emergency deployments, and citizens for personal safety precautions.

"Early warning saves lives. Every single degree of temperature we track helps us protect vulnerable citizens, construction workers, and children from life-threatening heat illness."
— Chief Meteorologist / State Disaster Management Directorate

Forecast & Alert Workflow (Ordered List)

  1. Collect live temperature and humidity feeds from automated weather stations.
  2. Analyze data for heatwave threshold anomalies and multi-day escalation trends.
  3. Generate predictive forecast models for vulnerable regional clusters.
  4. Issue color-coded alert bulletins (Yellow, Orange, Red) to disaster response units.
  5. Notify stakeholders via SMS broadcasts, push notifications, and public web dashboard.

Integrated Data Sources (Unordered List)

  • AWS Sensors: Micro-climate telemetry deployed across high-density urban grids.
  • IMD Database: Synoptic weather charts and historical climatological baselines.
  • Satellite Feeds: Geostationary thermal surface temperature mapping.

Stakeholder Hierarchy & Privileges (Nested List)

  • Meteorologists
    • View real-time AWS temperature and humidity telemetry
    • Run predictive algorithms and issue preliminary forecasts
  • Disaster Management Officers
    • Approve and escalate regional red alerts
    • Coordinate drinking water distribution and medical relief shelters
  • Citizens & General Public
    • Subscribe to automated SMS and email advisories
    • Access public safety manuals and hydration recommendations

Technical Abbreviations & Glossary (<dl>)

AWS
Automatic Weather Station — An automated version of the traditional weather station designed to save human labor and enable measurements from remote areas.
IMD
India Meteorological Department — The principal agency responsible for meteorological observations, weather forecasting, and seismology.
HW
Heatwave — A period of abnormally hot weather lasting several days, recognized when maximum temperature crosses 40°C in plains or 30°C in hills.
SHW
Severe Heatwave — Declared when maximum temperature departure from normal is 6.5°C or more, or when maximum temperature reaches 47°C.
