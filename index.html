Welcome to the Heatwave Intelligence Web Portal

Welcome to the Climate Intelligence for Heatwave Monitoring, Prediction and Early Warning Portal. This portal is utilized by meteorologists, disaster management officers, and citizens to track heatwave conditions, calculate thresholds, and coordinate rapid response measures.


The system connects IoT-enabled Automated Weather Stations (AWS), the India Meteorological Department (IMD) database, and localized satellite feeds to deliver timely life-saving bulletins.
